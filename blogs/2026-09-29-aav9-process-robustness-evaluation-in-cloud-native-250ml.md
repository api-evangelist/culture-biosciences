AAV production processes can be challenging to standardize, making process robustness a critical consideration as developers work toward scalable manufacturing. In collaboration with Thermo Fisher Scientific, Culture Biosciences evaluated the robustness of the Gibco™ AAV-MAX Helper-Free AAV Production System in cloud-native 250 mL bioreactors.
