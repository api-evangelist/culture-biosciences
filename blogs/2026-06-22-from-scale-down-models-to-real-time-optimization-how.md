Introduction Bioprocess development has been limited by slow experimentation, biological variability, and decisions based on retrospective analysis. Traditional approaches including, one-factor-at-a-time studies, disconnected data systems, and fixed process recipes, were designed for an era with fewer data and limited computational tools. Today, biopharmaceutical companies face increasing pressure to accelerate development while improving productivity, process understanding, and manufacturing readiness.
