# Culture Biosciences

> Culture Biosciences is a South San Francisco TechBio company that builds cloud-connected bioreactor systems and bioprocess development services for cell culture. Its Console cloud platform pairs automated single-use bioreactors (250mL and 5L, plus the Stratyx mobile bioreactor) with real-time monitoring, experiment management, data analytics, and remote collaboration, while its Nexxys services group runs clone/strain/construct screening, process characterization, process development, and bioprocess modeling for synthetic biology, protein therapeutics, and viral vectors. The company operates the Console cloud platform but does not currently publish a public developer API, OpenAPI, SDKs, or developer documentation.
